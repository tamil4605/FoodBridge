Simple 3-Step Process

How FoodBridge Works

Eliminating logistics delays by connecting surplus donors directly with local charitable recipients.

1

1. List Surplus Resource

Restaurants or donors publish surplus meals, rations, or materials with quantities, location, and safe collection deadlines.

2

2. Match & Request

Verified NGOs and orphanages browse nearby donations, submit quantity requests, and donors accept or coordinate schedules.

3

3. Pickup & Verification

Recipient collects donations at the scheduled time, both parties confirm handover on the platform, ensuring full transparency.
